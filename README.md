## 🎯 Project Overview

The Executive Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators.

The project focuses on analyzing **2,707 sales records** across 17 countries, 4 regions, 5 product categories, and 4 sales channels.

The dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor revenue, gross profit, sales volume, profitability, and geographical sales performance.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

## 🎯 Business Objectives

1. Monitor overall revenue, gross profit, and sales performance.
2. Identify monthly revenue and profitability trends.
3. Analyze the top-performing countries based on revenue.
4. Evaluate sales channel contribution to total revenue.
5. Compare gross margin performance across product categories.
6. Provide interactive reporting for business decision-making.
